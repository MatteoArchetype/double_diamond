# Cycle 171 · Review evidence while framing Define

In an open **Define** visit, select an interpretation under **Interpretations informing the frame**. The app shows its reviewed passages, evidence-fit status and linked uncertainties. A passage can be opened for assessment, and the unsaved frame is restored when returning to Research at the same case revision.

The view is advisory. Uncertainties still require separate selection in the frame. Saving the frame or approving a phase change remains an explicit action.
