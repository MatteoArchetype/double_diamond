# Cycle 155 · Publication search in Discover

Create a case with a question and choose **Find and review sources** in Discover. Enter public search terms and select **Search publications**. The app sends those terms to Crossref and displays up to eight bibliographic leads. Open the original via its DOI link. **Prepare for review** fills the title, publisher and URL in an empty review form.

Search results are temporary. Crossref covers registered publications, and coverage and relevance vary by topic. The manual source form also accepts material found elsewhere. Full text, exact passage, source context, scope fit and approval require human review. Search leaves the case and audit history untouched.

Browser verification used a fresh question about street trees and neighborhood heat in an isolated workspace. Crossref returned eight leads. Selecting one populated the source form with approval unchecked and the evidence list still empty.
