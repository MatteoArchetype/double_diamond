# Assess how evidence fits an interpretation

First retain and review a source, then explicitly link its passage to an advisory
interpretation. In **Evidence fit**, select that interpretation/passage pair. Unlinked
passages are not offered; an assessment cannot create the link or source evidence.
Inspect the exact passage and location before rating it.

The twelve existing dimensions are strength, directness, causal attribution,
transferability, independence, recency, context completeness, claim fitness, selection
risk, baseline quality, outcome validity and systemic relevance. Rate each Unknown, Low,
Medium, High or Not applicable. All start Unknown. These labels describe the named
dimension: High selection risk means high risk. They are not combined into a score and
are not a statistical measurement or independent validation. Unknown is a valid explicit
record; Not applicable requires your reason, not an automatic inference.

Record known limits, one per line, and explain the ratings. Empty limits means none
recorded, not proof that none exist. Save the advisory assessment explicitly. Even all
High ratings cannot accept, retain or promote a claim, change a human decision or validate
causation. Source/synthetic labels and source snapshots remain unchanged.

Saved ratings belong to exact versions of the linked interpretation and evidence. If the
claim changes, including its uncertainty context, the assessment becomes stale. The earlier
record is preserved. Review the changed research and record a separate new assessment.
The stale current panel labels the displayed interpretation as current context, not the
original assessed version; original versions remain in the audit. No history is overwritten.

Saved reports show assessments and freshness as recorded at report creation, with only
report-embedded interpretations/source passages. Matched at report creation does not mean
current against later research. Already stale at report creation remains explicitly labelled.
The report itself can also be historical/stale. No live context is silently substituted.

The UI does not edit/delete assessments, reconstruct an earlier assessed claim from its
fingerprint, infer truth, perform study validation or authorize external action. A deliberate
reassessment creates another record. Invalid, stale, unauthorized or interrupted submissions
leave no partial assessment. Check saved research before retrying uncertain responses.
Unsaved forms survive same-page/revision section navigation, not reload or closure.

The tool remains local/single operator. Independent source/reviewer authentication, actual
research, consequential claim disposition decisions, publication and final human acceptance
remain separate responsibilities. Use Double Diamond review gates for returns or reframing.
