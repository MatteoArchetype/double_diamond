# Record separate evaluations in Deliver

After reviewed progression through Discover, Define and Develop, open the active Deliver
visit. Select the alternative for the research decision. Record the method actually used,
choose its reviewed evidence, and state the outcome and limits. A planned experiment is
not a completed evaluation. Source and synthetic labels stay visible.

Choose **Add another evaluation** for a distinct method, observation or contrary result.
Each row can evaluate a developed alternative. The existing case allows up to 20 evaluation
rows. **Remove last evaluation** removes only the final unsaved row and its criterion links;
a new row with the same number does not inherit the removed row's links.

For every Define success criterion, select the evaluations that inform your assessment.
Only evaluations of the alternative selected for the research decision can be linked.
Changing that alternative or an evaluation's alternative clears now-ineligible links;
changing back does not silently restore them. Evidence and other evaluation rows remain.
Several evaluations may inform an unknown outcome when evidence disagrees. The tool checks
traceability, not scientific validity or whether a method was genuinely performed.

A met/not-met assessment requires a linked evidence-backed supported/unsupported evaluation
respectively. Unknown/not-met assessments need explicit next research. Recommendation needs
all criteria met; revise and stop-with-uncertainty remain explicit research decisions.

Save the Deliver record explicitly. Invalid or stale submissions cannot partially save the
record. Inspect full saved findings to see every method, outcome, source passage and linked
criterion in its retained context. Stop the pass explicitly before creating a report.
Reports, source selections and saved findings do not grant publication or external action.

Unsaved contents survive section navigation in the same page, but not reload or closing the
browser. Saved research remains immutable for that visit; request reviewed return to revise
it. The interface remains local/single operator; broader analysis editing, independent
identity/source verification and actual user acceptance remain open.
