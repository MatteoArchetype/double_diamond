# Cycle 168 · Compare evidence while drafting

In **Interpretations → Add a working interpretation**, choose evidence directly or bring selections over from **Find reviewed evidence**. **Compare selected passages** updates as the selection changes. It shows the exact passages, source locations, origin groups and labels for practice evidence. When selections share a recorded group, the view calls that out so it is easier to check whether the passages are independent.

The comparison is temporary and read-only. Group labels are recorded by researchers and do not establish independence. Interpretation wording and saving remain explicit research decisions.
