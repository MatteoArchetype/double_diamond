# Cycle 181 · Recover unfinished Develop and Deliver work

Use **Save draft for later** beneath an open Develop or Deliver form. **Open saved draft** restores alternative or evaluation rows, evidence links, criterion outcomes, next steps and the reason field. The current visit's existing form validation still governs recording the phase.

Drafts are stored on this computer for a specific open visit and excluded from case checkpoints. Saving or restoring a draft creates no finding, recommendation or approval. Saving a phase record closes access to that visit's draft.
