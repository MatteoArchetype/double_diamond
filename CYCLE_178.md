# Cycle 178 · Save an unfinished source review

In **Sources → Review a passage**, enter any part of a review and choose **Save draft for later**. After a reload or restart, choose **Open saved draft** to restore it. The form shows whether the entries differ from the saved draft. You can replace or remove the saved copy explicitly.

Each case has one saved draft for the standard source review form. It is stored locally outside the audited evidence and case checkpoint downloads. Drafts keep the synthetic label and never keep the approval checkbox. A saved draft remains available after evidence approval until replaced or removed. The inline saved-document approval form and other research forms are outside this feature.

If two views try to replace the same draft, the second write is refused. **Refresh saved draft** checks the newest version without replacing the open form.
