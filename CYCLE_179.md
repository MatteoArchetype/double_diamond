# Cycle 179 · Save unfinished Discover notes

In an open Discover visit, choose **Save draft for later** beneath the Discover form. **Open saved draft** restores the wording, selected evidence and reason after reload or restart. Drafts retain line breaks and are scoped to the exact open visit.

Draft saving is separate from recording a Discover finding or changing phases. A draft is excluded from checkpoint downloads. Once the visit is recorded or closed, its draft is unavailable from the current form. Source review drafts retain their existing behavior.
