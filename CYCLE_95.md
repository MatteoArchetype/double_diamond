# Cycle 95 — Claim Disposition

Claim dispositions convert evidence assessments into explicit, auditable belief revisions without treating them as automatic truth scores.

Supported dispositions:
- retain_bounded
- narrow
- split
- qualify
- promote_to_hypothesis
- unresolved
- withdraw

Foundational framing changes and consequential claims require human review. Dispositions retain prior status, new status, evidence IDs, rationale and material uncertainties.
