# Cycle 154 — visible initial Discover notes for actual research questions

Cycle 153 was a synthetic walkthrough on unchanged Cycle 152. This release adds a
conditional read-only panel for initial Discover notes stored in case metadata. It
separates source observations, source limitations, provisional interpretation and gaps,
links to real publications, and states that no source intake, phase decision or final
acceptance is recorded. Notes cannot confer approval: research writes still use existing
explicit source review and exact human gate boundaries. URL schemes are restricted to
HTTP/HTTPS and all text is rendered inert. Existing cases without a brief render as before.

Actual case briefs live outside this source archive in research-questions-workspace,
with one case for healthcare/AI and one for insurance middle-management work. They are
initial international public-source scans, not completed findings. US-specific NAIC
material is identified. Evidence about middle managers is indirect, and both cases remain
in Discover with no accepted evidence, saved phase record or human gates. Existing earlier
workspaces were not overwritten. The personal shortcut now selects the two-question workspace.

Metadata notes are preparatory annotations, outside the audited research-state snapshot.
They are not accepted ledger evidence, do not survive a DB-only checkpoint by themselves,
and must be preserved with their separate review packet. Do not treat them as authenticated
or immutable. Human review is needed before source intake and any later problem framing.
No independent identity/source authentication, primary interviews, empirical healthcare
validation or middle-manager employment forecast is claimed. Final MVP-08 acceptance
remains pending. See TEST_RESULTS.md and RELEASE_STATUS.json for actual validation.
