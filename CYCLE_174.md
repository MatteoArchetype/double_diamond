# Cycle 174 · Check Deliver criteria before saving

In a Deliver draft, **Check criteria and recommendation** updates as you enter reasons, next research steps and evaluation links. It flags missing reasons, missing next steps for unmet or unknown criteria, and absent evidence-backed evaluations for met or unmet outcomes. A draft recommendation also flags unmet or unknown criteria and missing recorded support for the selected alternative.

The check describes draft entries and links. It does not assess evidence quality, verify that an evaluation happened, or save the record. The existing save validation and human gates still apply.
