# Cycle 170 · Evidence fit review queue

Open **Evidence fit → Passages to assess** to see linked passages that need reassessment or a first assessment. Stale pairs appear first. Each item shows the interpretation, passage title, location and any synthetic label. Select **Assess passage from…** to open that exact pair in the assessment form.

The queue comes from current saved links and assessment freshness. Pairs with a current assessment stay in saved history and are omitted from the queue. A revised interpretation can make a previous assessment stale again. The queue is advisory, and ratings require an explicit save.
