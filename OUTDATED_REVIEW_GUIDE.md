# Review history after research changes

A phase or material-framing proposal is tied to the research context that existed when
it was made. Changing an interpretation can make that proposal outdated. A historical
approved status does not renew itself when the research changes.

In Research, navigation counts only pending/approved proposals whose saved context
matches the current research. A notice counts outdated open reviews separately. In
Approvals, outdated reviews retain their recorded status, original proposal and reason,
but have no approve or apply controls. A pending outdated proposal may still be explicitly
rejected with a reason. A previously approved decision is not silently rejected or deleted.

For an outdated problem frame, interpretation and uncertainty identifiers are shown as
original references. Current wording is not presented as the wording used in the old
review. The preceding-frame comparison is omitted when its context is outdated.

Review current research, reconsider affected findings in a separately reviewed visit if
needed, then request a new exact proposal. Approval and application remain separate.
Matching context alone never promises application: visit budgets, current saved findings,
phase rules, authorization and exact core gate checks still apply at the write boundary.

All status checks are read-only and recomputed after restart/checkpoint recovery.
Restoring a checkpoint preserves decisions and does not create new approval. Legacy
proposals without the required context binding are treated as outdated by this interface.
Core gate records, journal, workflow rules and source integrity remain unchanged. Browser
controls do not grant final MVP acceptance, publication or external execution. Unsaved
browser drafts still do not survive reload. No real research or acceptance is inferred.
