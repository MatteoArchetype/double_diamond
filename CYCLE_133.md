# Cycle 133 — carry the research question through to the decision

Built from unchanged Cycle 132 with 745 passing baseline tests. New version 3 cases
connect Define's stated success criteria to Deliver's selected alternative, evaluations
and evidence. Every criterion is assessed with a reason; failures/unknowns name the
next research action. A recommendation cannot bypass unfulfilled criteria.

This advances Double Diamond research decisions: Define establishes what matters,
Develop explores competing explanations, Deliver states what the evaluations support,
and iteration follows the remaining gaps. It does not turn a stored outcome into
independent experimental validation. Synthetic evidence remains explicit.

Versioned replay preserves older cases, approvals and report hashes. An unchanged
Cycle 132 checkpoint is included as a genuine compatibility fixture. The actual public-
source case remains paused with its human decisions and final acceptance pending;
continued building did not approve or migrate it. See CRITERIA_GUIDE.md and TEST_RESULTS.md.

Limits: quality of criteria, evidence and judgments remains a human research task;
there is no automatic experiment executor, finished graphical interface, external
publication, hardware power-loss validation or actual final host acceptance. Passing
fixtures does not complete the product or imply a guaranteed cycle countdown.
