# Keep each alternative's basis separate

After a reviewed transition from Define, open Develop. Record at least two competing
explanations or options. Each alternative has its own explanation, interpretations,
reviewed source passages, proposed test, and limits/assumptions. Choose at least one
interpretation explicitly for each; passage selection may be empty when no evidence is
linked. An empty list is retained as empty, not filled from another alternative.

Selecting an interpretation does not auto-select the evidence behind it. A claim can
inform an option without every attached passage being used as that option's explicit
basis. The tool records these separate choices; it does not infer support, independence,
scientific validity or a preferred explanation. Synthetic source labels stay visible.

Use Add another alternative within the case's existing budget. A new row has no inherited
text or selections. Remove last alternative removes only the final unsaved row, keeping
at least two. Re-adding its number creates a fresh blank row. No saved finding is edited.

A proposed test describes what you would investigate. It is not an actual evaluation,
completed experiment or permission to contact people, retrieve sources or execute work.
Deliver separately records evaluations actually carried out and criterion assessments.

Save the Develop record explicitly. Validation or stale-revision failure cannot partially
save alternatives. Unsaved row contents and links survive section navigation/error rendering
within the same page/revision, not reload, closure or changes to saved research. Inspect
the full saved Develop visit to see each option and its original interpretation/source
context. Later edits do not substitute into earlier findings. Reports/checkpoints retain
these records; research data needs its own checkpoint, not just a code ZIP.

Saved findings are immutable for that visit. Request reviewed return to revise them; a
changed Define frame still requires its existing exact human review. No phase progression,
claim acceptance, disposition change, research validation, publication or final human
acceptance is inferred from saving alternatives. The tool remains local/single operator.
