# Cycle 175 · Trace criteria to evaluations and sources in saved reports

In **Reports**, open **Inspect evaluations and evidence** beneath a success criterion. The panel shows the recorded evaluation method, outcome, limits, and retained source passages from that report. It uses the saved report snapshot.

Reports created in this release also preserve evidence linked directly to evaluations, even when it was not linked to an interpretation. The readable export includes those additional passages. Older reports retain their original content. Some older reports include references whose passage text was not retained. The app does not substitute current evidence for missing historical text.
