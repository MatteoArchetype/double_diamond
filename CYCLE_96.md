# Cycle 96 — Contradiction-Driven Synthesis

Contradictions are first-class research records. The runtime requires at least
two evidence items, an issue, comparability assessment, assessment of the
conflict, consequence, next action, and audit trail.

Unresolved/material contradictions prevent an unqualified claim. Context,
definition, timing, or method differences require qualification rather than
being treated as genuine contradiction. No majority voting is used.
