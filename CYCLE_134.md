# Cycle 134 — turn unresolved criteria into the next research

Built from unchanged Cycle 133 with 773 passing baseline tests. The research tool now
saves a proposed agenda from the evidence gaps in a current criterion-linked report.
Items carry criterion/evidence lineage, a question, method, return phase, stated priority
and reason, effort, dependencies and a signal of progress. All unmet criteria must be
covered; contradictory IDs, cyclic dependencies and unbounded plans fail.

This closes the planning connection from Deliver's unresolved judgments back to
Discover/Define/Develop. It does not perform that research, complete a task, approve
primary research or silently reframe the question. Every item remains explicitly
proposed and unexecuted. Priorities are planning judgments, not invented certainty.

Agendas retain immutable context, with stale marking after research/workflow changes.
Separate gate reviews neither alter the research basis nor grant agenda execution.
Read-only inspect/Markdown/JSON export and checkpoints preserve the existing integrity,
authorization and recovery boundaries. Previous case/report hashes remain supported;
a genuine Cycle 133 checkpoint is tested. No actual-case approval or migration occurred.

See RESEARCH_AGENDA_GUIDE.md, PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md and TEST_RESULTS.md. Real research
inputs, host decisions, validity of methods and the unfinished interface remain open.
No real study, external execution, publication or actual final host acceptance is claimed.
