# Cycle 176 · Plan follow-up from a report criterion

In **Reports**, select **Plan follow-up for this criterion** on an unmet or unknown criterion in a current report. The app opens **Next research** and focuses that criterion's draft. Its recorded next step and gap provide starting text. The plan still includes every unresolved criterion in that report.

Drafts are kept separately by report and case revision while the page is open. Historical or stale reports cannot start a new agenda. Saving creates a proposed agenda only and does not authorize or execute research.
