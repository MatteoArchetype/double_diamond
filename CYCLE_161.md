# Cycle 161 · Search across saved Word documents

Open **Sources → Saved Word documents** and enter a word or phrase in **Search saved documents**. Search is case-local and ignores capitalization. It shows up to ten matching main-body paragraphs with filename and paragraph number, and reports documents whose searchable text was unavailable or incomplete.

Use **Open saved document** to inspect the original's text preview or **Use passage and details** to draft a source review. Searching and drafting leave the evidence ledger and Double Diamond phase unchanged. Original source checks and approval remain required.

Only saved DOCX files in the current case are searched. Matching is literal and reads supported main-body text. The app does not generate an answer or send document contents to an external service.
