# Cycle 156 · Source shortlist

In **Discover → Find and review sources**, search for publications and choose **Keep on shortlist** beside a promising result. Each case has its own shortlist. It survives app restarts and can hold up to 100 DOI links. Use **Prepare for review** to copy publication details into an empty review form or **Remove from shortlist** to remove a lead.

Shortlisting records a publication lead outside the evidence ledger. Open the source, check the passage and its context, and explicitly approve it to save it as evidence. The shortlist stays in the local workspace and is excluded from case checkpoint downloads.
